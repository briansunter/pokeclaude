### Ruler of the Skies (B4) — settled

- **Hoopa ex** — the set's defining card: 1-Energy Shadow Bullet (30+20 bench chip) sets up Cyrus; Dynamite Punch 100 w/ 20 recoil. Three Hoopa decks sit in the top 18.
- **Vespiquen ex + Combee** — Chase Order self-discard (140, dodges Cyrus, no points given) became an A-tier archetype with Shuckle ex.
- **Mega Rayquaza ex + Dragonair** — Mega Burst (50× per discarded R+L Energy) with Dragonair's discard-recovery ability: B-tier, rising.
- **Mega Sharpedo ex** — burst onto A-tier at launch (Turbo Shark 70 + bench accel), now settling (↓9).
- **Mega Manectric ex + Heliolisk / Minior / Rainbow Cave / B4 Helioptile line** — solid mid-high additions.

## Matchup Matrix

**Favorable:**

```
Psychic (Mega Altaria ex / Indeedee ex) → Fighting (Mega Lucario ex — explicitly its worst matchup)
Lightning (Magnezone / Miraidon ex)     → Water (Suicune / Milotic / Mega Sharpedo)
Water (Suicune / Milotic / Chien-Pao)   → Fire (Mega Blaziken / Mega Charizard Y)
Grass (Mega Sceptile / Vespiquen ex)    → Water (Suicune / Baxcalibur)
Fire (Mega Blaziken ex)                 → Grass (Sceptile / Vespiquen / Butterfree)
Fighting (Mega Lucario ex)              → Darkness (Hoopa ex / Mega Absol ex / Team Rocket's Weezing ex)
Darkness (Hoopa ex / Mega Absol ex)     → Psychic basics without partners
```

**Difficult:**

```
Fighting  ← Psychic (Mega Altaria ex + Espeon remains the Lucario wall)
Water     ← Lightning (Magnezone + Miraidon ex is the format's fastest riser)
Fire      ← Water
Grass     ← Fire (Mega Blaziken ex is back on top)
ex-heavy boards ← Oricorio walls (tech Minior to answer it)
```

## Rising Strategies

- **Turbo evolution openers.** Caterpie's Quick Growth (self-evolve from deck when it survives a turn Active) makes Butterfree a turn-2 130HP 1-point attacker; Quick-Grow Extract accelerates the back row (Butterfree + Mega Sceptile ex is the #1 deck).
- **Self-discard value.** Vespiquen ex's Chase Order turns benched basics into damage (140) — no points conceded, no Cyrus targets left behind. Fragrant Forest refuels the fodder.
- **Punish-the-ramp attackers.** Indeedee ex (+30 per energy on the opponent's Active) and Mega Lucario's 140 threshold prey on the energy-hungry meta; Magnezone+Miraidon ex (57.7%) is the pure ramp payoff.
- **Split/recoil chip.** Hoopa ex Shadow Bullet (30 + 20 to bench for 1 Energy) manufactures Cyrus kills; Mega Lucario ex + Hitmonlee/Bonsly replicate the pattern in Fighting.
- **Status lock.** Team Rocket's Weezing ex (Poison + Burn + Confused), Milotic ex Water Pulse sleep, and Espeon/Igglybuff sleep + Darkrai all punish slow boards — but dedicated sleep stall (Igglybuff builds) is now F-tier; status works as tempo, not as the win condition.
- **Watch list:** Indeedee ex + Giratina ex (62.6% WR) needs a bigger sample before it's crowned; Magnezone + Miraidon ex (57.7%, ↑8) is the safest riser.

## Declining Strategies

- **Mega Altaria ex core slipped** (pure ↓10): the shell still wins via Espeon/Happiny partners, but Iron Valiant / Gourgeist variants are gone from the top tables.
- **Suicune ex + Baxcalibur** (↓6, ~47.0%): the B3b-era S deck is now outpaced by Milotic builds and Lightning ramp.
- **Mega Sharpedo ex + Gyarados** (↓9) and **Hoopa ex + Mega Absol ex** (↓4) are settling after hot B4 launches.
- **Mega Sceptile ex + Sceptile** fell to F (↓24) — the Butterfree and Greninja variants replaced it.
- **Hype flops to avoid:** Mega Diancie ex (F), Mega Gardevoir ex pairings (F), Hisuian Zoroark ex (no-show), Garchomp variants (D/F), Team Rocket's Weezing ex beyond the Hoopa pairing (F).
- Giratina ex + Darkrai ex, the long-time standby, collapsed to F-tier.
